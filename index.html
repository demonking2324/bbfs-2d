HOW TO FLY
  1. Take off: set takeoff flaps (A320: 1, 737: 5, 747: 10, 727: 15), throttle to TOGA, and pull up gently at rotate speed (about 140–158 kt) to about 10° (9° in the 737, 8° in the 727). The A320 and 747 tails strike at about 12°, the 737 at about 11°, the long 727 at about 10.7°. Gear up once climbing.
  2. Climb & cruise: retract flaps and set CL thrust. Tap AP to open the autopilot: set SPD and ALT, then engage AP and A/THR. Speed up time with 1× above 2,500 ft.
  3. Descend at the T/D marker on the profile display: lower the ALT target (or idle thrust and nose down by hand) to about 250 kt.
  4. Land: slow down, landing flaps (A320: FULL, 737: 30, 747: 30, 727: 40), gear down. Arm APPR and the autopilot follows the glideslope; it disconnects at 100 ft so you flare by hand. Throttle idle, then the brakes stop you.

Keys: W/S pitch · ↑/↓ throttle · G gear · F/R flaps · Z speedbrake · B brakes · A autopilot · Q autothrust · L approach · P autopilot panel · T sim rate · Space pause

Fly-by-wire: release the stick and the aircraft holds its flight path. Below 50 ft it holds attitude, so you flare. Moving the stick or throttle disconnects the autopilot or autothrust.

Before departure the load sheet sets passengers and fuel: heavier means a longer takeoff roll, higher speeds and more fuel burn. Watch the maximum landing weight and keep the reserve.
